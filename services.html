01 · Interior Remodeling

Kitchens, baths, and everything between

Most of our interior work starts by opening something up — a wall, a floor, a shower that was never built right. We take it down to framing when the job needs it, move plumbing and electrical where it should have gone, and rebuild with tile, trim and cabinetry that lines up.

Tub-to-shower conversions, full bath gutting, closets turned into working pantries, doors that finally close, backsplashes, vanities, finish carpentry. Small jobs too — we do the ones other contractors skip.

02 · Additions

More house, tied in right

An addition is only as good as the seam where it meets the house. We dig and pour footings, frame, and tie the new roofline into the existing one so the finished house doesn't read as two houses.

Bedrooms, sunrooms, three-season rooms, screen porch additions over new slab. We pull the permits, schedule the inspections, and match the siding and shingles so it looks like it was always there.

03 · Outdoor Living

“Turning Porches Into Paradise”

Outdoor living isn’t an afterthought for most coastal homeowners — it’s where people cook, relax, entertain, and unwind. Our outdoor projects are built with long term performance in mind, using materials that hold up to moisture, UV exposure, and heavy use while still looking sharp.

All across the Grand Strand, every outdoor space has different needs: airflow, bug control, shade, privacy, and weather resistance. We approach outdoor living with a builder’s mindset and a homeowners point of view, creating spaces that are practical, usable, and built to last.

The Breeze Boys, LLC. offers a wide variety of different outdoor living solutions. From covered pergolas with motorized screens, to custom Ezebreeze or Glass windows, we have you covered.

Not only can these upgrades make you the envy of your neighborhood, but they can also add long-term value to the home by improving functionality, livable footprint, and market appeal—especially in coastal areas where outdoor lifestyle is a major selling point.

04 · Hurricane Protection & Security

Storm-Ready. Security-Focused. Style-Driven.

Storm protection should work for you even when the weather is clear. Our Bahama Screens and Magnatrack systems are built to withstand coastal conditions while also enhancing your home’s security, comfort, and aesthetic appeal. Whether it’s a stray golf ball, storm debris, or even acts of vandalism, these systems add a serious layer of protection where it matters most.

Beyond impact protection, these systems offer everyday benefits that homeowners appreciate year-round. They help reduce UV exposure that can fade flooring and furniture, provide additional shade for cooling efficiency, and may even qualify for certain storm-mitigation incentives or insurance considerations. Added protection and improved curb appeal can also enhance resale value — making this a smart upgrade now and a strong selling point later.
