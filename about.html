How this started

Between us it's years of framing, finish carpentry, concrete and storm work on the coast. We'd both spent enough time on other people's job sites to know exactly what goes wrong: the contractor who stops answering, the price that moves, the crew that leaves a mess in your driveway for a week.

So we started The Breeze Boys to run it the other way. Aiden handles estimates, permits and the schedule. Robert runs the crew and the build. When you call, you get one of us — not an office, not a call center.

We're still small on purpose. It's how we keep timelines realistic and keep our name on every job that leaves our hands.
